iPhone Manager - what gets installed
====================================

On the next pages you can choose what to install. Everything is selected by
default:

  * iPhone Manager itself (required).

  * ffmpeg - plays iPhone videos (HEVC) inside the app. Downloaded during
    setup from the official Windows builds (gyan.dev, about 115 MB).

  * Apple Devices - Apple's free app from the Microsoft Store. It brings the
    "Apple Mobile Device" driver and service that every iPhone tool on Windows
    needs. Installed through winget; Windows may ask for permission. This item
    only appears when the driver is missing (iTunes, Apple Devices or iMazing
    already provide it).

After setup, connect the iPhone by USB, unlock it and tap "Trust this computer".

iPhone Manager runs a local web server on http://127.0.0.1:8765 and opens it
in a Microsoft Edge app window. It is free software under the GNU GPL v3 or later.
ffmpeg is licensed under the GPL; Apple Devices under Apple's own terms.
