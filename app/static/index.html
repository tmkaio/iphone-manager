    Backups

    Entire iPhone

    Everything on the iPhone: messages, contacts, photos, app data, settings. Same format as iTunes and Finder. After the first one, only what changed is copied. Restore it after a recovery, a failed update or on a new iPhone.

    Only some data

    Saves just this part, to bring it back later without touching anything else. For an app's data, use Back up next to the app in Apps.

    Saved backups

    WhatiPhoneDateSize
    How restoring works. iOS only restores whole backups, so to bring back one app, Messages or Contacts the app first backs up the iPhone as it is now, puts the saved part in place of the current one and restores that: everything else stays as it was. Before restoring, turn off Find My (Settings › your name › Find My) and keep the cable connected; the iPhone restarts at the end. With Messages, Contacts or Notes in iCloud, iCloud syncs its own copy back afterwards.

    Buttons

    Send text

    Click = tap; click and drag = swipe. With the mouse over the screen, the PC keyboard types on the iPhone (Enter, Backspace, arrows…). Accented letters don't go through the keyboard: the iPhone receives a US keyboard.

    Update iOS

    Update, not restore. Your data, apps and photos are kept. Only versions Apple still signs that are the same as or newer than the current one () can be installed. Back up first, and keep the cable connected and the computer on throughout.
